## Description

Merchant

Commercial Register

business sector

An electronic service provided by the Ministry of Commerce through the Saudi Business Center platform, allowing beneficiaries to start their businesses without having to visit service centers.​

Service level agreement

1. Access the platform: Via the National Unified Access through the Saudi Business Center platform.
2. Select the service: From "Electronic Services" > "Commercial Registration" > Select the registration type "Establishment", then start the service.
3. Fill in the establishment information: Review and agree to the terms. - Fill in the owner's information and contact information. - Enter the approved business address.
4. Add the required information: Select the business activities. - Enter the e-commerce information (if applicable). - Determine the capital. - Choose the trade name type and its composition.
5. Complete the commercial registration information: Enter the establishment's address and contact information. - Appoint the establishment manager and enter their information.
6. Review and submit the application: Review the application summary. - Approve the declaration and submit the application.
7. Party approval: If required, approval will be obtained from the register owner or other managers.
8. Payment: Issue the invoice and pay the fees via SADAD or card. - After payment, the commercial registration is officially issued.
9. Automatic registration with relevant authorities: After issuing the record, registration is automatic with: the Ministry of Human Resources - the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority - the General Organization for Social Insurance - Saudi Post (National Address) - the Chamber of Commerce.

1. Must be at least 18 years old.
2. Not a government employee.
3. The owner must not have an active commercial registration for a sole proprietorship.

1. Attach the approval of the licensing authority if the activity being practiced is one that requires a license prior to issuance.
2. Specify the practice of e-commerce if the applicant is practicing it.
3. If the applicant is a cooperative or charitable association, the following documents must be submitted from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development or the National Center for Non-Profit Sector Development: - Attach a copy of the registration certificate. - Attach a copy of the letter approving the association's board of directors, along with a copy of the association's bylaws. - Attach a copy of the cooperative association's bylaws. - Attach a power of attorney from the association's board of directors to a Saudi person to complete the registration procedures in the commercial register.
4. Verify the following if the applicant is an endowment institution: - Attach the endowment deed from the Personal Status Court. - Attach a legal power of attorney from the donor, the endowment supervisor, or the supervisory board to issue a commercial registration with the endowment deed.
