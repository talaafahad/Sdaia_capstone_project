# Mobile Cart License Issuance

## Mobile Cart License Issuance

Start Service

* Mobile Cart Licenses
* Individuals
* Incubator
* Portable cart
* Investor

An electronic service available on the Balady platform that allows individuals to start their business by issuing a mobile cart license without the need for a commercial registration or visiting the authority.
Once the mobile cart license is issued, the Freelance Work Certificate will also be automatically issued.

service level agreement

Privacy and Terms of Use

1. Access the service.
2. Fill in the license application details.
3. Pay the required fees.
4. Confirm and submit the license request.

Service Level Agreement

1. Must be a Saudi national.
2. Must be 18 years old or older.
3. Must not be a government employee.
4. Must not be on the municipal suspension list.
5. Must not belong to restricted tribes as per regulations.
6. Must not already hold a mobile cart license.
7. Must not hold a retail outlet license.
8. Must not hold a commercial license.

Is the freelance work certificate issued proactively upon the issuance of a mobile cart license?

Yes, the freelance work certificate is issued proactively after completing the mobile cart license issuance request and settling the invoice.
