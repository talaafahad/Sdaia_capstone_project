An electronic service provided in my platform through which you can start to practice business through the issuance of a commercial activity license, in addition to the safety permit issued by the General Directorate of Civil Defense for some commercial activities approved by the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing, the fast track aims to provide a exceptional experience in the stage of issuing licenses through partnership with engineering offices designated and approved for commercial license services, without the need to visit the municipality.

Privacy and Terms of Use

You can apply for a business license for your activity, including but not limited to a business license, environmental license, industrial company license, medical company license, food company license, tourism company license, professional organization license through the following steps:

1) Enter the establishment registry and specify the activity and space
2) locate and fill the details of the shop
3) identify the engineering office and send the application

4) Payment of fees after approval of the application

##

When can an objection be filed against the suspension or cancellation of a license, and what is the time limit for objection?

An objection can be filed after the license has been suspended or canceled by the municipality/secretariat, and the objection must be submitted within 60 days from the date of suspension or cancellation.

##

What should I do if I receive a notification stating "Commercial License Number is Incorrect"?

If the license is linked to a Commercial Register, log in as Owner/Establishment Manager. If the license is linked to a National ID, log in using the "For Myself" option.
